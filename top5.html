    Deckhand (Seasonal), WG-6

    National Park Service · Salem, MA or International Falls, MN (Voyageurs)

    $27.58–$33.38/hr · federal job · May–Oct 2027

    Why you

    • This is the best-paying boat job you're qualified for right now: about $28–33/hr, roughly double what a dockhand makes.
    • It doesn't ask for a degree, a TWIC or a Merchant Mariner card. They grade you on job elements like small boat handling and vessel upkeep, and your 1,000+ helm hours and 7 seasons running charters cover that.
    • Federal seasonal work on your resume makes the next federal boat job (NOAA, Army Corps, MSC) a lot easier to get.

    The catch

    It doesn't start until May 2027, so it won't pay the bills this winter. Apply anyway: it takes 30 minutes and closes tomorrow. On USAJobs, describe each boat job in detail (hours, group sizes, docking, fueling). A short resume gets screened out.

    Boat Assembly Technician (Mechanical & Electrical)

    Zodiac Nautic · Summerville, SC (Charleston area)

    $20–$25/hr · full time · entry level

    Why you

    • It's year-round boat work that pays $20–25/hr. Marina jobs are dead until spring, and this keeps your career in boats going through the winter.
    • You'd install engines, controls, steering and gauges. Knowing how those parts are built makes you a better operator, and it gives you a straight path to marine technician, which is the hardest-to-fill job at every marina.
    • You've lived and worked in Charleston before (CofC, HPS valet), so moving there isn't starting from zero. It also clears your $20+/hr rule for the Charleston area.

    It's 2 hours from Myrtle Beach, so you'd have to move. Factory work: standing all day and lifting up to 50 lb. They "prefer" 1–2 years of mechanical experience. It's not required, so list any boat maintenance you did at Pinecrest.
